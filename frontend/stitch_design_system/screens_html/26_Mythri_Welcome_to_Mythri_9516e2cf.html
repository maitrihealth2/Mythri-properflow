Our Sanctuary Principles

What to expect inside Mythri

Mythri offers an attentive, reflective ear whenever your mind feels heavy or words need a safe harbor to settle.

  • • Never rushed: Take five seconds or twenty minutes to reply. Silence is welcome here.
  • • Strict confidentiality: Your reflections are isolated and not sold or traded.
  • • Supportive boundaries: If you are experiencing extreme distress or danger, we gently guide you to qualified human emergency professionals.
